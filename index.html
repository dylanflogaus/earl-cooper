About Earl Cooper

Faith, Family, and Service

Guided by faith, committed to family, and ready to fight for Delaware. Reverend Earl Cooper brings a community-first leadership style focused on opportunity and truth. He believes leadership starts with listening—on the doorstep, at town halls, and in Congress—and turning what he hears into honest, practical action. From stronger public schools and support for local entrepreneurs to protecting the dignity of Delaware seniors, Earl is focused on the issues that shape everyday life across the First State.
