About Earl Cooper

Called to Serve Delaware

Reverend Earl Cooper leads with conviction, community commitment, and a Delaware-first perspective focused on real outcomes for families and seniors.

Rev. Earl Cooper's Story

Earl Cooper's mission is rooted in faith, family, and the belief that leadership should always be accountable to the people. His campaign centers on practical action to improve schools, support entrepreneurs, and protect the dignity of Delaware seniors.

"I'm here to serve Delaware with faith, truth, and courage - let's stand together."

Through local engagement and direct problem-solving, Earl has worked to help residents find jobs, stabilize families, and build stronger neighborhoods.
