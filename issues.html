Standing for Delaware

Campaign Priorities

Real priorities for real families: student protection, stronger communities, and dependable senior support across every county in Delaware.

Protect Our Kids

Our children are our greatest treasure, and if we do not protect them we fail the very future of Delaware.

  • Protect parental rights and school transparency.
  • Keep educational environments focused and safe.
  • Support youth mentorship and faith-based outreach.

Community & Small Business Growth

Work hand-in-hand with local communities using practical intervention and entrepreneur support to remove barriers to growth.

  • Expand neighborhood-level business support programs.
  • Lower friction for local startups and family businesses.
  • Prioritize jobs and workforce pathways for Delaware residents.

Commitment to Senior Access

On day one, launch a person-to-person Senior Access Hotline so every senior can speak to a real person about concerns.

  • Live support for benefits and urgent assistance referrals.
  • Reduce wait times and unnecessary paperwork burdens.
  • Improve accountability for senior service delivery.

Delaware First Values

Practical Action Over Political Theater

Earl Cooper's issue agenda is focused on measurable outcomes: stronger families, safer communities, fair opportunity, and direct constituent service.

The campaign approach is simple: listen first, then solve with urgency and integrity.
